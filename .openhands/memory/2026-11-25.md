
## Plans became trades

- His exact words split cleanly into "stop X" and "make Y happen". I did X by
  moving a default (not by unregistering the loop -- a feature that is off by
  default AND unregistered can never come back), and Y by splitting detection
  from execution.
- The design question I had to answer myself: does an armed plan go through
  `plan_check`? No. Scenario 2 IS the counter-trend buy that `plan_check`
  refuses, and refusing it would mean the bot quietly ignores a scenario the
  owner wrote. The armed plan is a decision about WHEN; the gates about HOW MUCH
  and HOW MANY still apply.
- Mutation-checked both rules: restoring the push default to "1" fails the pin;
  moving the take above the kill switch fails the silenced test.
- End-to-end proof I ran before pushing: fired plan -> trade row
  `(short, 3535.00, stop 3543.00, target 3517.40, note 'plan:1')` -> card ->
  second pass on the same bar returns None.
