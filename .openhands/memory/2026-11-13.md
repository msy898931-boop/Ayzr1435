## The entry BASIS: no trade in the middle of the range (the owner's core rule)

- ⭐ **The owner's rule, finally enforced in the engine:** the desk may NOT take a
  market entry except on a BASIS — either a **rejected bounce off a level** (بيع
  الارتداد) or a **confirmed break** (بيع الكسر بإغلاق ساعة). Never an immediate
  entry in the middle of the range. This is what he means by "act like me".
- Implemented as a switch `ENTRY_BASIS_ON` (env `DESK_ENTRY_BASIS`, default ON),
  same shape as `FILTERS_ON`/`NOCHASE_ON`. New producer `_entry_setup(direction,
  live, res, sup, b1=...)` returns `{kind, level, entry}` only when the last
  closed 1h bar shows one of the two bases; else `None`. `_no_setup_detail()`
  names BOTH bases needed, owner-facing.
- ⭐ **The gate is the LAST gate in `plan_check()`** so it does not MASK a more
  specific reason (quality/filter, no-chase). Order matters: a refusal must name
  the most useful cause, and "no basis" is the fallback, not the headline.
- ⭐ **A retrace requires the price to still BE at the level.** First cut let a
  break setup quote BASIS retrace at a level 10 pts away — exactly the
  middle-of-range entry the owner forbids. Tightened: retrace only when price is
  AT the level; otherwise it is a chase, not a bounce.
- ⭐ **The wall trade is its OWN basis, exempt from the gate.** A wall entry IS a
  rejection (the last 1h poked the 38.2% fib and closed back). Gating it would
  remove a trade the owner already accepts. `plan()` fires on a wall regardless
  of `ENTRY_BASIS_ON`.
- ⭐ **Isolation tests must toggle the new gate OFF.** Old tests assumed `plan()`
  fires on a frame-agreed trend with no basis — precisely the behaviour the owner
  removed. Added `bot.ENTRY_BASIS_ON = False` (save/restore) in FilterStatusTest,
  NewsTest, NoChaseTest, OpinionTest, and PlanCheckTest's clean-long.
- New `EntryBasisTest`: refuse mid-range (reason `no_setup`, detail names
  "نص النطاق"), accept a rejected retrace, accept a confirmed break, refuse a
  retrace the price has left, wall path exempt, switch-off restores immediate
  entry.
- ⭐ **One more contradiction fixed while wiring the cards:** `_verdict` carried
  the fib **wall** even when the frames AGREED on a direction — so `card_action`
  said "wait for the wall 3,896.25" while `plan()` (basis met) said "sell now".
  The wall is a basis ONLY for the no-confirmed-trend case; now gated on
  `up < CONFIRM_FRAMES and dn < CONFIRM_FRAMES`.
- Cards now name the basis: `_headline`/`_verdict_detail`/`card_action`/
  `card_recommend` show the `no_setup` detail; `_why_taken` leads with
  "أساس الدخول: كسر X بإغلاق ساعة" or "ارتداد لـ X ورفض".
