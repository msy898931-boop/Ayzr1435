## MarketDesk — the bot was silent: a port bound too late (2026-11-07, night)

- ⭐ **A Render web service must bind its port BEFORE anything that can block.**
  The owner said "the bot isn't working". `getUpdates` returned a *real update*
  (not the 409 that proves a poller is attached) and `/health` showed
  `uptime_s: 8` — the service was restarting. The logs told the whole story:
  `12:20:08 Running 'python3 app.py'` … **8 silent minutes** … `12:28:23 Running
  'python3 app.py'` again, and then
  `Port scan timeout reached, no open ports detected`.
  Cause: `main()` called `restore_journal()` — a Telegram round trip — *before*
  opening the socket. On a slow network the port did not exist when Render
  scanned, so Render killed and restarted the process, forever. **Serve first,
  do everything else after.** The fix moved `serve_forever` to the very top of
  `main()`; startup then logged `marketdesk up: health on :10000/health` 3 s
  after `Running 'python3 app.py'`, and the port-scan timeout never returned.
- ⭐ **Diagnose "bot is silent" in this order:** (1) `getUpdates` — a 409 means a
  poller holds the lock, a *result* means nothing is polling; (2) `/health` — a
  small `uptime_s` means it restarted; (3) Render logs for `Port scan timeout`.
  That trio localised this in one pass, after I had wrongly suspected the
  webhook and the pinned-message read.
- **Restore off the critical path.** `restore_journal` now runs in its own
  thread with a bounded `join(60)`, so a slow Telegram can delay the journal but
  never the bot.
- **Supervise every loop.** A daemon thread that dies silently is how a bot
  becomes a mystery; `_supervise` restarts any loop that returns or raises.
  `poll_forever` also survives a network error and a single malformed update.
- **A liveness endpoint must not block.** `/health` now answers even if the DB
  is busy (`open_trades` → -1 on error): a health check that can block can fail
  the deploy.
- **`getWebhookInfo.pending_update_count: 0` is the cleanest proof the bot is
  consuming.** Better than a 409, which only proves *something* holds the lock.
- Final: commit `16bf16a` live, `/health` uptime rising monotonically 6→240 s
  with no reset, `pending_update_count: 0`, port bound in ~3 s, no port-scan
  timeout.

  not a check.**
